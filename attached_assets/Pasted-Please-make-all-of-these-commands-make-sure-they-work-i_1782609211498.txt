Please make all of these commands, make sure they work in the command bar when you press ; also ask me anything you don't know or for extra information for specific commands. For the sm and im commands, look at the photo below to see how the text/font should be like.

Read up the whole structure from the repo and to see everything so you know what you're dealing with.

blind - Blinds players
createcorpse - Creates a corpse 
unblind - Unblinds players
blindAll - Blinds everyone
bring - Brings a player to you
chatlogs - Opens chatlogs 
clearhats - Clears a players hats (accessories) 
clearwaypoints - Clears any current waypoints
countdown - Provides a countdown on the left side of the screen
damage - Damages a player
esp - Shows player name, username, health, and other stats
fly - E to fly, alt to speed up, flies the player put in the parameter
giveitem - Permanently gives a player an item
hat - Gives a player a hat
heal - Heals a player
help - Sends a help message to Staff who has helpUI on
helpUI - Use if help commands aren't showing, test with help 
invis - Makes your character invisible 
uninvis - Make your character visible 
kick - Kicks a player from the server
music - Plays a music id
notif - A modified pm that shows who sent it at the bottom of your screen
place - Places players to a place id
pm - A private message that appears on the other player's middle screen, fades in and out
privateserver - Creates a separate instance that requires you to drag players to it
ragdoll - Ragdoll
re - Refreshes a player and places them back to where they were
removehat - Removes a players hat
removeitem - Permanently removes a players items
respawn - Respawns a player to world spawn
serverbring - Brings a player to your server
serverjoin - Join another players server
setwaypoint - Sets a waypoint down for players to see
setworldspawn - Sets the world spawn elsewhere
shutdown - Shuts the server down 
sm - A server message utilized as a primary function for narration in game, shows on the bottom of the screen, fades in and out 
im - A specific message for one individual, shows at the bottom of the screen and fades in and out
tempitem - Temporarily gives a player an item 
to - Teleports you to a player
tp - Teleports player 1 to player 2 
unblindAll - Unblinds everyone
watch - Watches a different players pov
